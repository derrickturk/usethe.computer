% I'm Sorry, Slash Goggles

<p>I was a kid in a truck<br>
and the cows were upright, which<br>
meant that hydrogen sulfide levels<br>
were acceptable</p>

<p>And on my BlackBerry I could read<br>
Lambda the Ultimate at 9600 baud,<br>
which isn't right, but it's how<br>
I remember it, just like the rest</p>

<p>And one day just below Frank Atanassow<br>
(I wonder where he went, I hope he's OK)<br>
fan fiction and Cylons and the Frankfurt school<br>
had snuck into our little garden</p>

<p>I thought, what a crock, "critical code studies",<br>
these guys need to learn how things work<br>
and lay off the Lacanian crap<br>
and leave us all alone</p>

<p>I'm so sorry,<br>
the silence is so much worse</p>
